# Journal Characteristics: MS&E Top Journals

This file provides detailed characteristics for each journal, organized by tier. Each entry covers: editorial philosophy, departmental structure (for MS), paper structure norms, methodology preferences, reviewing standards, and distinguishing features.

---

## Tier 1: UTD-24 Flagship Journals

### Management Science (MS)

**Publisher**: INFORMS
**Editors-in-Chief**: Multiple departmental editors
**Acceptance Rate**: ~8-10%
**Typical Review Rounds**: 2-3

#### Editorial Philosophy

The unifying thread: "an analytical focus on improving the understanding of management." MS accepts papers that are (1) relevant to management theory or practice, (2) meet high standards of rigor, and (3) are of broad interest.

From the Optimization & Decision Analytics department: "Papers leaving a lasting impact tend to offer simple and robust solutions and contain sharp and insightful messages."

#### Departmental Structure (17 departments)

MS is organized into departments, each with its own Department Editor and editorial statement:
1. Accounting
2. Behavioral Economics and Decision Analysis
3. Data Science
4. Entrepreneurship and Innovation
5. Finance
6. Healthcare Management
7. Information Systems
8. Market Design, Platform, and Demand Analytics
9. Marketing
10. Operations Management
11. Optimization and Decision Analytics
12. Organizations
13. Stochastic Models and Simulation
14. Strategy
15. Sustainability

**Where OM papers go**: Primarily Operations Management (OM), Optimization and Decision Analytics, Stochastic Models and Simulation, or Market Design.

#### Key Features

- **Healthcare Management** requires a nontechnical executive summary (max 250 words) identifying the intended practitioner audience and key practical implications
- **Data Editor** enforces reproducibility: authors must provide data, code, and materials
- **Engagement and Practice Impact Editor** manages a Practice Advisory Board that writes commentaries on selected articles
- **Double-anonymous review**; authors can suggest reviewers and AEs
- **Reject-and-resubmit** policy: if resubmitting a previously rejected MS paper, authors must disclose this and justify

#### Paper Structure Norms
- Total length: ~38 pages (body) + unlimited appendix
- Abstract: 200 words max, unstructured, single paragraph
- The OM department prefers: Motivating example → Model → Analysis → Extensions → Numerical studies → Managerial insights
- Managerial insights are **mandatory** for OM papers
- Use of comparison/gap tables in literature review is standard

#### What MS Values Most
1. **Novelty of the problem**: A problem not previously studied in the MS&E literature
2. **Sharpness of the insight**: A clean, intuitive result that changes how managers think
3. **Broad appeal**: The paper should interest scholars beyond the specific sub-area
4. **Rigor**: Complete, correct, well-structured analysis

#### Common Desk Rejection Reasons
- Incremental extension of an existing model
- Problem is too narrow in scope
- Managerial motivation is weak or fabricated
- Paper fits better in a field journal

### Operations Research (OR)

**Publisher**: INFORMS
**Typical Review Rounds**: 2-4

#### Editorial Philosophy
OR is the premier outlet for **methodological advances** in operations research. Unlike MS, which emphasizes broad management relevance, OR values **deep theoretical contributions** to optimization, stochastic modeling, and decision analytics.

#### Methodology Focus Areas
- Linear, integer, and nonlinear optimization
- Stochastic programming and robust optimization
- Dynamic programming and Markov decision processes
- Game theory and mechanism design
- Queueing theory and stochastic processes
- Simulation methodology
- Machine learning for OR

#### Key Features
- Proofs are expected to be **complete and self-contained**
- Algorithmic contributions require computational complexity analysis and approximation guarantees
- Numerical experiments are **secondary** to theoretical results — they validate, not substitute
- Literature review is often more technically focused than in MS
- Managerial insights are appreciated but **not required**

#### What OR Values Most
1. **Theoretical depth**: A new methodology, proof technique, or analytical framework
2. **Technical correctness**: Flawless proofs and rigorous analysis
3. **Methodological generality**: Results that apply broadly, not to one narrow setting

#### Paper Structure Norms
- Formulation → Structural Properties → Solution Method → Theoretical Guarantees → Computational Validation
- Proofs in body or appendix based on length
- Parameter tables and sensitivity analysis in computational section

### Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (MSOM)

**Publisher**: INFORMS
**Typical Review Rounds**: 2-3

#### Editorial Philosophy
MSOM is the leading journal for **empirically-grounded operations management** research. It emphasizes the intersection of analytical modeling with real-world data and behavioral experiments.

#### Key Features
- **Empirical rigor is paramount**: Papers without data or behavioral experiments face an uphill battle
- Behavioral operations experiments are a distinctive MSOM strength
- Models should be **motivated by and validated with data**
- Managerial insights must be specific, actionable, and grounded in the data
- Encourages papers on: sustainability, healthcare operations, platform operations, supply chain innovation

#### Paper Structure Norms
- Motivating empirical observation → Model (driven by data patterns) → Analysis → Empirical validation → Managerial implications
- The link between data and model must be explicit: "Our data shows X, which motivates our modeling choice Y"
- Robustness checks with alternative data sources or model specifications

#### What MSOM Values Most
1. **Empirical grounding**: The paper starts with data and ends with data-validated insights
2. **Managerial relevance**: Clear implications for operations managers
3. **Methodological pluralism**: Analytical models, structural estimation, field experiments, lab experiments all welcome
4. **Relevance to practice**: Connection to real operational challenges

### Production and Operations Management (POM)

**Publisher**: Wiley (Production and Operations Management Society)
**Typical Review Rounds**: 2-3

#### Editorial Philosophy
POM publishes research on the design, management, and improvement of production and service operations. It is the broadest of the top OM journals in terms of topic coverage.

#### Key Features
- Welcomes both analytical and empirical papers
- Strong emphasis on **supply chain management**, **sustainability**, and **service operations**
- Special issues are common and well-regarded
- Data-driven papers increasingly preferred
- Managerial insights expected

#### Paper Structure Norms
- Similar to MS but slightly more flexibility in structure
- Empirical papers may use IMRAD-like structure
- Special issue papers follow guest editor guidelines

#### What POM Values Most
1. **Timely topics**: Papers addressing current operations challenges
2. **Practical relevance**: Results that can improve operations practice
3. **Methodological rigor**: Appropriate methods applied correctly
4. **Contribution clarity**: Explicit statement of what is new

## Tier 2: Strong Field Journals

### Transportation Science (TS)

**Publisher**: INFORMS
**Focus**: Transportation systems, logistics, network modeling

**Key characteristics**:
- Deep theoretical and methodological contributions to transportation
- Network optimization, traffic assignment, vehicle routing, public transit
- Strong proof expectations; rigorous algorithm analysis
- Real transportation data is highly valued
- Papers often include case studies with real transit networks

**Paper structure**: Model → Properties → Algorithm → Computational study on benchmark networks → Case study

---

### Transportation Research Part B (TRB)

**Publisher**: Elsevier
**Focus**: Methodological advances in transportation

**Key characteristics**:
- The most methodology-focused transportation journal
- Mathematical rigor is essential
- Papers without proofs are rarely accepted
- Network equilibrium, dynamic traffic assignment, transit scheduling
- Less emphasis on managerial insights than TS; more on mathematical contribution

---

### Decision Sciences (DS)

**Publisher**: Wiley (Decision Sciences Institute)
**Focus**: Decision-making processes and methodologies

**Key characteristics**:
- Decision analysis, multi-criteria decision making, AHP/ANP
- Behavioral decision research
- Supply chain decision-making
- Welcomes analytical, empirical, and experimental methods
- Explicitly interdisciplinary

---

### OMEGA - The International Journal of Management Science

**Publisher**: Elsevier
**Focus**: Concise, high-impact management science

**Distinctive feature**: **Brevity**. OMEGA papers are typically ~6,000 words. The journal explicitly seeks papers that communicate their contribution efficiently.

**Key characteristics**:
- "Short and sharp" philosophy
- Managerial insights are **critical** — more important than theoretical novelty
- Welcomes all OR/MS methodologies
- Strong emphasis on real-world applications and case studies
- No long derivations in the body; proofs go to appendix or supplementary materials
- Preferred paper: clear problem → simple model → strong data → actionable insight

**Types of submissions**:
- Research papers (~6000 words)
- Short communications (~3000 words)
- Review papers (by invitation)

---

### Transportation Research Part E (TRE)

**Publisher**: Elsevier
**Focus**: Logistics and transportation management

**Key characteristics**:
- Supply chain logistics, freight transportation, shipping
- Combines transportation with broader logistics concerns
- Empirical papers are common
- Less theoretically demanding than TS or TRB
- Managerial implications for logistics practitioners

---

### European Journal of Operational Research (EJOR)

**Publisher**: Elsevier (EURO - Association of European Operational Research Societies)
**Typical Review Rounds**: 2-3

#### Editorial Philosophy
EJOR is the **most methodologically inclusive** top OR journal. It publishes across the full spectrum of OR methods, from theoretical optimization to soft OR.

#### Submission Types
1. **Invited Review**: Comprehensive surveys of an OR area (by invitation only)
2. **Innovative Applications of OR**: Real-world applications with documented impact
3. **Theory and Methodology**: New OR methods with rigorous validation

#### Key Features
- **Welcomes heuristic and metaheuristic methods** more than US journals
- **Multi-criteria decision making (MCDM)** is a distinct EJOR strength
- European research tradition: strong on applications, less on pure theory
- Numerical experiments must be thorough: multiple benchmark sets, statistical testing, sensitivity analysis
- Managerial/practical implications expected

#### Paper Structure Norms (Theory & Methodology)
- Introduction (with clear contribution statement) → Literature review → Problem description → Model/algorithm → Computational experiments → Conclusion
- Algorithm papers must include: pseudocode, complexity analysis, parameter calibration, benchmark comparison

#### What EJOR Values Most
1. **Rigorous methodology**: Correct, well-validated methods
2. **Computational thoroughness**: Extensive numerical testing
3. **Practical relevance**: Connection to real problems
4. **Clarity**: Well-written, well-structured papers

#### Common Categories
- **Production, Manufacturing and Logistics**
- **Stochastics and Statistics**
- **Decision Support**
- **Discrete Optimization**
- **Continuous Optimization**
- **Data Science and Analytics** (newer area)

---

### International Journal of Production Economics (IJPE)

**Publisher**: Elsevier
**Focus**: The intersection of production/operations and economics

**Key characteristics**:
- Manufacturing and service operations with economic analysis
- Empirical papers using econometric methods are common
- Supply chain management, inventory, sustainability
- **Strong data orientation**: papers without real data face difficulty
- Managerial implications are emphasized
- Less mathematically demanding than MS/OR

**Paper structure**: Typically follows MS pattern but with heavier empirical component

---

### International Journal of Production Research (IJPR)

**Publisher**: Taylor & Francis
**Focus**: Production engineering, manufacturing systems

**Key characteristics**:
- Engineering-oriented, less theoretical than MS/OR
- Manufacturing process design, scheduling, quality control
- Industry 4.0, smart manufacturing, digital twins
- Welcomes case studies and industrial applications
- Algorithm-heavy: heuristic methods are standard
- Practical implementation details valued

---

### Computers & Industrial Engineering (C&IE)

**Publisher**: Elsevier
**Focus**: Computational methods for industrial engineering

**Key characteristics**:
- **Algorithm and computation focused**
- Metaheuristics (GA, PSO, ACO, SA, TS), machine learning for IE
- Simulation modeling (discrete-event, agent-based)
- Digital twin, cyber-physical systems
- Less theoretical than any other journal on this list
- Emphasis on computational performance and practical applicability
- Papers are shorter (typically 20-25 pages)

**Paper structure**: Problem description → Literature review → Proposed algorithm/method → Computational experiments (extensive) → Conclusions
